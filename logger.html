Source and phone

Source buttons send opcode 0E. Drop phone audio disconnects the phone's audio link; with an AUX cable in, the amp then plays AUX by itself. It only works while the amp is on Bluetooth.

Alternative source commands

The vendor app uses these instead of opcode 0E. On the ZK-502MT they behave the same, and AUX is still forgotten on power loss. If you find a way to make the amp start on AUX, please open an issue.

USB / Music

For boards with a USB port added. The ZK-502MT normally has none, and its Music source answers with an error. "Watch" re-reads the online-device bits (attribute 2) every second and logs every bit that changes, plus every source change, with the time, so dropouts and resets show up in the log. A GATT disconnect is logged too.

Read (no changes)

Replies to the Wuzhi reads arrive as pushes, decoded in the log. LED gets no reply and DSP an empty one on the ZK-502MT. The decoded line hides the pairing code, but the raw RX line shows its 4 digits; exported logs redact it unless you tick the box.

Timing

How long to wait in situations where the amp or the phone needs time to react. Saved in this browser and used by the app too (reload the app after saving). Values are clamped to sane limits.

Device identity
SDK probes (read-only)

Commands from docs/SDK_EXTRAS.md §2 that the vendor app never sends. None changes a setting. The ZK-502MT answers none of them usefully (§6). "Run all" sends them one after another and writes a summary to the log.

Commands with no use on the ZK-502MT

Reboot: no reply and no effect. Power off leaves the amp half-asleep (no source switching) until you power-cycle it.
